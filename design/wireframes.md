# MEC Homepage Wireframes (High-level)

1 — Hero (Full-screen)
- Background: cinematic video loop (muted, optimized), animated floating shapes
- Left/center: primary headline, subheading, CTAs (Enroll / Tour / Watch)
- Right: animated stats, micro-CTA

2 — Virtual Classroom (scroll-driven)
- Simulated classroom scene with chalkboard as text panel

3 — Wall of Achievement
- Grid of certificate frames, hover reveal

4 — Corridor Doors (Academic Levels)
- Interactive doors open to previews

5 — Science Lab (interactive)

6 — Digital Learning Centre (ICT lab)

7 — School Life (notice-board cards)

8 — Trophy Cabinet (student success)

9 — Meet the Teachers (staff room UI)

10 — Parents Lounge (testimonials)

11 — Virtual Library (shelves and clickable books)

12 — Gallery (polaroid wall)

13 — News Board (pinned notices)

14 — Admissions Reception UI

15 — Contact & Footer

UX Notes:
- Each section is a self-contained 'scene' with unique visual language.
- Use ScrollTrigger to animate transitions between scenes.
- Provide a compact mobile experience: stacked content, simplified interactions.
