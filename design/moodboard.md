# MEC Homepage Moodboard

Concept: A premium, warm, and academic experience that reads as a virtual campus tour. Visual language mixes glassmorphism, soft gold accents, royal purple primary, deep-blue anchors, and generous whitespace.

Primary colors:
- Royal Purple: #7720E9
- Deep Blue: #0B2B5A (suggested)
- White: #FFFFFF
- Light Grey: #F4F6F8

Accent colors:
- Soft Gold: #D4AF37
- Sky Blue: #62B6F0

Typography:
- Heading: 'Inter', 'Poppins' or 'GT America' (bold, geometric, modern)
- Body: 'Source Sans 3' or 'Roboto' (readable, neutral)

Imagery:
- Cinematic hero video (students, campus life, natural light)
- High-quality portraits for teachers
- Environmental shots for campus areas (labs, corridors, fields)

Motion:
- GSAP-driven scroll transitions
- Micro-interactions (magnetic CTA, hover tilts)
- Three.js for subtle 3D objects (floating books, DNA helix)

Accessibility & Performance:
- WCAG AA contrast on text
- Optimized video (WebM + MP4 fallbacks)
- Lazy-loading, responsive images

Notes:
- Keep interaction optional (sound off by default)
- Mobile-first simplified interactions
